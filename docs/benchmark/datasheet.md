## Evidence and privacy

- Gold answers are documentation answers; runtime observations confirm or contradict them and are stored separately.
- Documentation-conflict items are a diagnostic slice, excluded from headline metrics.
- Release-post and release-note text (restricted) is never stored in the benchmark or sent to any external model; items cite restricted sources by path and line only.

## Known limitations

- Version-specific (T2) items are scarce: most version changes are not documented in each version's own pages (protocol v1 §1).
- Documentation corrections (earlier docs disagreeing with that version's behaviour) are excluded from counted items.
- Canonical observations were recorded and validated for all probes referenced by the benchmark items. Automatic per-item comparison against runtime observations is not implemented. Items are verified against documentation and owner review; probe consistency remains pending.
- Two T2 drafts held for a suspected documentation conflict were released into the review by owner decision D2 and reviewed with the pilot.
- Drafted with a Gemini free-tier model; only owner-accepted (V2) records are gold.
